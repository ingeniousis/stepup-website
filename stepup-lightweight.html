FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do I organize a step challenge on StepUp?

Create a group from StepUp settings, share the invite link by email or text, and members will appear on shared daily, weekly and monthly leaderboards. Admins can also set a fixed date range challenge.

Is StepUp really completely free?

Yes. StepUp is free and nothing is behind a paywall. The app supports itself through minimal ads, optional ad-free purchases and supporter coffees.

How many people can join a group?

Groups can have up to 1,500 members, and you can create as many groups as you want.

Can I run a fixed date range challenge?

Yes. Admins can set a challenge start and end date from group settings. The custom leaderboard can cover date ranges from 1 day to 1 year.

How do group reports work?

Group admins can export reports for any date range. Reports include total steps, total distance, average steps per member and detailed step leaderboards.

Which wearables are supported?

StepUp supports wearables that sync with Apple Health on iOS or Health Connect on Android, including Apple Watch, Fitbit, Samsung, Garmin, Oura, Whoop, Withings, Xiaomi, Android Wear and many more.

Will StepUp drain my battery?

No. StepUp uses the phone's built-in step sensor and does not need GPS to count steps, so it is battery friendly.

Who can see my steps?

By default, no one can see your steps. Friends you add and groups you join can see relevant step activity. StepUp does not post or share your steps anywhere.

How do I delete my StepUp account and data?

You can delete your account and data immediately from StepUp app > Settings > Help and feedback > Delete account.
