FAQ

Answers organizers search for.

This prototype uses visible FAQ content plus FAQPage structured data. In the real site, each answer can also link to deeper markdown guides.

How do I organize a step challenge on StepUp?

Create a group, invite people with the group link, and StepUp automatically fills the leaderboard as members join. For fixed challenges, admins can set a custom start and end date from group settings.

Is StepUp really free?

Yes. StepUp is free to use. The app supports itself through minimal ads and optional in-app purchases for people who want to go ad-free.

Can StepUp handle workplace challenges?

Yes. Groups can include up to 1,500 members, use leaderboards and chat, and export reports for winner announcements or monthly recaps.

Which wearables does StepUp support?

StepUp syncs through Apple Health on iOS and Health Connect on Android, which covers Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin, Samsung and many other devices that write steps to those platforms.

Who can see my steps?

By default, no one can see your steps. Friends you add and groups you join can see relevant step activity. You can block people and delete your account and data from the app.

Will StepUp drain my battery?

No. StepUp uses the phone step sensor and synced wearable data. It does not need GPS to count daily steps.
