How to run a free step challenge with StepUp

Create a group, share an invite link, and everyone competes on live daily, weekly and monthly leaderboards. Free for everyone, on iPhone and Android.

{% include download-buttons.html t=t dark=false %}

How a StepUp step challenge works

StepUp is used for step challenges at work (Amazon, BMW, Google), at schools (Yale, Stanford), and by gyms, clubs and groups of friends around the world. Setting one up takes a few minutes. Organising one for your company? See our workplace step challenge guide.

  1. Create a group

    Download StepUp and create a private group for your team, class, club or friends.

  2. Invite people

    Share the group's invite link by email or text. People tap it to install StepUp, then tap it again to join.

  3. Everyone walks

    Steps count automatically from each person's phone or wearable. Daily, weekly and monthly leaderboards update on their own.

  4. Celebrate winners

    Announce weekly or monthly winners, cheer each other on in group chat, and export reports for any date range.

Choose your step challenge format

Ongoing monthly challenge

The simplest option. Every group has daily, weekly and monthly leaderboards that run all the time, so you can announce winners every week or every month.

Daily and weekly leaderboards reset at midnight. The monthly leaderboard switches to the new month on the 3rd, so everyone can still see last month's results for the first two days.

Fixed date range challenge

Great for events like a "Walktober" or a 30-day challenge. Admins set custom leaderboard dates from Group Settings (long-press the group name) > Custom leaderboard dates.

Members then see the custom leaderboard in place of the monthly one. Ranges can be anything from 1 day to 1 year, and the group continues as normal afterwards.

Team vs team challenge

Create one group per team, such as by office, country or department, and have each person join only their team's group.

Export each group's report to compare total steps or average daily steps per member, then share the standings by email. Each group can have the same admin or its own. See more workplace challenge ideas.

Mystery Mode

In Mystery Mode, today's step counts are hidden and only ranks are shown. Steps are revealed the next day.

It keeps the competition friendly and works especially well for work groups. Admins can turn it on from Group Settings.

Tips for a successful step challenge

Invite people early

Send invites at least a week before your start date so everyone has time to install StepUp and connect their wearable. Steps recorded after someone joins show up on the group leaderboards straight away.

Keep prizes inclusive

Prizes are fun but not essential: recognition and bragging rights are often motivating enough. Prizes only for the most steps can create unhealthy competition at the top and discourage everyone else.

A good alternative is a random draw among everyone who reaches a goal, such as 10,000 steps a day or 300,000 steps in a month.

Keep the conversation going

Group chat lets members share updates, encourage each other and enjoy some friendly banter. Admins can turn off chat or taunts from Group Settings, and remove messages or members if needed.

Help people sync their steps

StepUp works with any iPhone or Android phone, and with wearables that sync to Apple Health or Android Health Connect. If someone's steps look low, see how to sync wearables.
