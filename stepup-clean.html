Frequently Asked Questions

How do I organise a step challenge on StepUp?

StepUp makes it super simple to host fun, engaging step challenges for FREE!
You can add friends individually or create a group where everyone can see each other on a shared leaderboard.
StepUp is used at work (Amazon, BMW, Google), schools (Yale, Stanford), gyms and communities all over the world, and we're excited for you to join the movement!

How does a group work?
In a group, all members can see each other on the group leaderboards (daily, weekly and monthly).
Groups can have up to 1,500 members, and you can create as many groups as you want. Invite people by sharing the group invite link via email/text.
Create a test group and invite a friend/colleague to try it.
Or join our "StepUp In 2026" group: https://join.thestepupapp.com/nJq00b

How much does it cost?
StepUp is completely FREE.
We want to start a global movement that's accessible to everyone.
We support our work via minimal ads, in app purchases to go ad-free and some coffees from generous supporters.

Leaderboards and ongoing challenges
Many groups run ongoing challenges where winners are announced every week or month.
By default, groups have daily, weekly, and monthly leaderboards that update automatically.
Daily and weekly leaderboards reset every midnight. The monthly leaderboard resets to the new month on the 3rd of the month. More guidance and email templates.

NEW: Fixed date range challenges
If you want a challenge with a specific start and end date, admins can set that up from Group Settings (long-press the group name).
Members will then see the new custom leaderboard in the group in place of the monthly leaderboard.
The minimum date range is 1 day, and maximum is 1 year. More guidance and email templates.

Does the group end after the end date?
No. The group continues as normal with its members and leaderboards. After the end date, the custom leaderboard will still show results for that selected date range (until an admin changes or clears the custom dates).

How to “start” a step challenge?
There's nothing to start or stop in the app once the group is created - since the leaderboards update automatically.
Make sure to invite folks a few days (at least a week) before the intended "start date" so they have time to install StepUp.
As people join the group, their recorded steps will appear on the leaderboards.

Prizes & kudos
Prizes can be fun—but often just recognition and kudos are even more motivating.
If you're planning prizes, note that a prize for "most steps" can sometimes create unhealthy competition at the top and discourage everyone else. Consider wholesome alternatives like:
 - random draws among all the folks who hit some goal (e.g. 10k steps everyday or daily avg)
 - prizes for most improved (highest % increase in steps over previous period)
Note: StepUp doesn't sponsor prizes - that's up to challenge organizers.

NEW: Mystery Mode in groups 🎭
In Mystery Mode, today's steps are hidden & only ranks are shown on the leaderboard. Step counts are revealed the next day. It's great for work groups with extra privacy during the work day & added suspense.
Admins can enable Mystery Mode from Group Settings (long press on group name).

Team vs team step challenge
StepUp doesn't currently show group-vs-group leaderboards directly in the app, but there's an easy workaround:
Create one group per team, then compare teams using exported group reports (total steps or average steps per member). Share the winner and stats over email/text. More guidance and email templates.

Exporting group reports
Group admins can export reports for any date range from Settings > Manage Groups (or long press the group name).
Reports include total steps and distance covered by the entire group, daily avg steps per member for the group and detailed step leaderboards.

Group chat
Group chat lets members share updates, encourage each other, and enjoy friendly banter.
Admins can disable chat (so only admins can post) or disable taunts from Group Settings (long press on group name).
Admins can delete any inappropriate message by long pressing it.
Admins can remove any member or make them an admin by long pressing them from the group leaderboard.

More information
You can create a group from StepUp > Settings > Manage groups > Create group.
Admins can rename or delete the group from Group Settings (via long press on the group name).
Admins can long press on any member in the group leaderboard to remove them, or make them an admin.
Members can leave the group (long press on the group name), or delete their account and data at any time.
StepUp is available in ALL countries worldwide.

How do I sync steps from wearables like Apple Watch, Fitbit etc? What devices are supported?
Sync wearables to StepUp from StepUp > Settings > Sync wearable.

You can sync steps from any wearable via Apple Health on iOS or Health Connect on Android:
Step 1: Sync StepUp with Apple Health/Health Connect from StepUp > Settings > Sync wearable
Step 2: Sync steps from your wearable/app to Apple Health/Health Connect

We also support syncing directly with your Fitbit, Garmin or Samsung wearables.

ALL wearables that can sync steps with Apple Health/Android Health Connect are supported including Apple Watch, Fitbit, Samsung, Garmin, Oura, Whoop, Withings, Xiaomi, Mi, Android Wear, Amazefit, Noise, boAT, OPPO and many, many more.
We cannot list them all, but if your wearable syncs with Apple Health/Android Health Connect, it will sync with StepUp!
How do I sync steps from my wearable to Apple Health?
On iOS, you can sync steps from any wearable from StepUp > Settings > Sync wearable.

You also need to sync steps from your wearable's app to Apple Health.
eg. Sync steps from Garmin via Garmin App > Settings > Connected Apps > Apple Health

Apple Health uses a priority system when reading data from multiple sources.
If your wearable data isn't appearing even though sharing is enabled, you might need to adjust Apple Health's data source priority to ensure your wearable is ranked higher than other conflicting sources.
You can do that from Apple Health > Steps > (scroll down) Data Sources & Access > (scroll down) Data Sources > Edit (top right) > Tap on ☰ on the right to drag your wearable to the top.
Can I manually add steps? (No)
StepUp works with all iPhone & Android smartphones, and also all wearables that can sync with Apple Health (iOS) or Android Health Connect.
But we don't allow manually added steps to prevent misuse.
How do I add friends in StepUp?
Sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook and send friends your unique invite link. You will see them in StepUp once they tap your invite link and accept the invite.
Note: If your friends are not on StepUp yet, the invite link will take them to the app store to install it. Then they need to sign in on StepUp and tap your invite link again to add you as a friend.

If you sign in with Facebook, you can automatically see your FB friends who have also signed in with FB on StepUp. You can still invite other friends who are not on FB via your unique invite link.

You can control who can see you in StepUp.

NOTE: We never post or share your steps data anywhere.
Who can see my steps?
By default, no one other than you can see your steps in StepUp.
We NEVER post or share your step data anywhere.

If you add friends, then only your friends will be able to see you & your steps.
If you join a group, then other people in the group will be able to see you & your steps.

You can privately hide (block) anyone on StepUp and then they won't see you in StepUp, and you won't see them.
How do I hide (block) friends in StepUp?
You can privately hide any of your friends in StepUp. You won't see hidden friends, and they won't see you in StepUp.
You can "unhide" them at anytime from StepUp > Settings > Hidden friends.
My friends or groups in StepUp disappeared. Help!
This shouldn't happen, but if it does, please check your internet connection to make it's working. Then try signing out of StepUp (Settings > Sign out), and signing back in. That should fix the issue. If that doesn't work, please email us at team@thestepupapp.com.

Note: If you're seeing some friends in StepUp but missing just one specific friend, it's possible that that person deleted their StepUp account, or hid you on StepUp.
What are Active Bot and Chill Bot? Can I hide them?
Active Bot & Chill Bot are virtual friends in StepUp that walk about 10K & 2K steps respectively.
Many users like to pace themselves against them, and enjoy the gamified competition & motivation.

You can hide (block/remove) them if you'd prefer, by tapping on them, and tapping on "Hide" button in the top right.
Will StepUp drain my battery? (NOT at all!)
StepUp uses your phone's built-in step-sensor/fitness tracker, which works in the background without impacting battery.
Since StepUp doesn't use your GPS location, it is very battery friendly! :)
How do I delete my StepUp account & data?
You can delete your account and data immediately from StepUp app > Settings > Help & feedback > Delete account
